## Photos

Every category is pictured on **https://tracrentaruba.com/vehicles**. When a customer asks
to see a car, link that page and name the category to look at.

The agent cannot send images, and must not describe colour, interior, trim or condition —
none of that is published, and the listing is "or similar" anyway.

## Key facts

- **Every vehicle category has air conditioning.**
- Models are listed as **"or similar"** — the website guarantees the *category*, not the
  exact model. Always phrase it as "a Kia Rio or similar."
- The Kia Sonet is presented as well suited to island exploring.
- The Hyundai Tucson is headlined "SUV" but described in the body text as a "full-size SUV."
  Prefer the neutral wording "SUV" when answering, to avoid setting a wrong expectation.
- The Hyundai Staria's 3-piece luggage figure applies **when all 11 seats are in use**.
  Capacity with fewer passengers is not published — do not estimate it.

## Choosing a vehicle (safe guidance)

These recommendations follow only from the published passenger/luggage numbers:

- **Couple or small family, light luggage** → Compact (Kia Rio) or Full Mid-Size (Kia K3)
- **Want a higher-riding small SUV** → Subcompact SUV (Kia Sonet)
- **5 people with more luggage (6 pieces)** → SUV (Hyundai Tucson)
- **6–7 people** → SUV 7-seater (Honda BRV)
- **8–11 people** → VAN (Hyundai Staria), noting the luggage limit at full occupancy

## What is NOT published about the fleet

Do not state or guess any of the following — hand off instead:

- **Transmission (automatic or manual)** — not stated for any vehicle
- Rental rates for any category
- Model year, fuel type, or fuel economy
- Number of doors
- Whether a specific vehicle is available on given dates
