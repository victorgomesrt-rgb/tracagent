# Extra charges and add-on services

> Source: tracrentaruba.com/extra-charges · Status: VERIFIED from the official website
> These are the **only prices published anywhere on the Trac website.**

## Child safety seats

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Infant car seat | $5 per day |
| Toddler car seat | $5 per day |
| Booster seat | $5 per day |

- **Installation:** "Customers are responsible for installing the car seat themselves, in
  accordance with Trac Car Rental policy."
- Availability is not guaranteed — see the reservation note below.

## Luggage transport

- **Drop-off** — luggage taken **from the airport to your designated location**: **$30**
- **Pick-up** — luggage collected **from your location and delivered to the airport**: **$30**
- The page also describes this as "a $30 one-time fee."

> ⚠️ **Known ambiguity — do not resolve it yourself.** The page states a "$30 one-time fee"
> *and* lists drop-off ($30) and pick-up ($30) as separate items. It is **not clear whether a
> round trip costs $30 or $60.** If a customer asks about round-trip luggage transport, give
> the per-direction figure ($30 each way as listed) and hand off to a human to confirm the
> total. Never assert a round-trip price.

## Reserving extras

Customers are asked to **add their selection to the reservation in advance to ensure
availability.** Extras are not guaranteed on the spot.

## Currency note

The website displays these prices with a "$" symbol but **does not state whether this is USD
or Aruban florin (AWG).** Quote the figure as written ("$5 per day") and do not name a
currency. If a customer asks explicitly, hand off.

## What is NOT published

- Additional-driver availability or fee
- GPS / phone holder / cooler or any other add-on
- Airport delivery **of the car itself** (only *luggage* transport is published)
- Hotel or resort delivery of the car
- Cleaning, late-return, or refuelling fees
