# Booking, payment, airport and delivery

> Source: **Trac owner**, supplied 2026-07-25 as their current manual reply script.
> Status: **CONFIRMED BY THE BUSINESS** — authoritative, and outranks the website where they
> differ. These are the answers staff give today.

## Reservation

- Reservations are made through the website: **https://tracrentaruba.com**
- **Booking is completely free of charge.**
- **Payment and deposit are made on arrival**, at the office — or when Trac delivers the
  vehicle to a cruise terminal, resort, hotel or Airbnb.
- Reservations are **recommended**: availability changes quickly and Trac is frequently sold
  out in high demand.

## To quote a customer, you need

Trac cannot quote without dates and times. Always collect:

- 📅 Pick-up date and 🕒 pick-up time
- 📅 Return date and 🕒 return time

## Payment

| | |
|---|---|
| Reservation | **Free** |
| When | **On arrival** (office, or at delivery) |
| Accepted | **Cash, Visa, Mastercard** |
| Card surcharge | **4%** on card payments |
| Not accepted | **AMEX, Discover** |

> ⚠️ Note the distinction: Visa and Mastercard **are accepted as payment**. The website's
> insurance page separately refuses Visa/Mastercard *insurance coverage*. These are two
> different things and must never be conflated.

## Deposit

- **Standard rental:** a **refundable deposit** is required.
- **Full Risk:** **no deposit** required.
- The deposit **amount** is not stated. Do not quote a figure.

## Office hours and out-of-hours fees

- **09:00 – 18:00**
- **Before office hours: $50 service fee**
- **After office hours: $50 service fee**

## Airport service — Queen Beatrix International (AUA)

- The office is **2 minutes from the airport**.
- **Free airport shuttle**, included.
- Trac **monitors the customer's flight**.
- **One day before arrival**, Trac emails the meeting location and instructions.
- Airport **self-return before office hours requires Full Risk** insurance.

## Delivery and pick-up — all free

| Location | Delivery | Pick-up |
|---|---|---|
| Hotels | **Free** | **Free** |
| Resorts | **Free** | **Free** |
| Airbnb | **Free** | **Free** |
| Cruise terminal | **Free** | **Free** |

## Returning the vehicle

- Normally returned **at the office**.
- After return, a **free shuttle back to the airport** is provided.

## Luggage transport

Optional, for when luggage does not fit in the rental vehicle. **Charged per trip.**
(The website lists $30 per direction — see `03-extras-and-services.md`.)

## Reputation

Trac is one of the highest-rated car rental companies in Aruba, with **thousands of 5-star
reviews**. Mention this naturally if a customer is comparing or hesitating — never as a hard
sell.

## What Trac does around the rental (context, not things to promise)

These are Trac's internal service steps. Do not promise them as guarantees, but they explain
messages a customer may receive:

- A **video of the vehicle** is sent to the customer.
- On **Full Risk**, photos of the fuel gauge are taken and sent. If the customer buys
  **prepaid gas**, no fuel photos are needed.
- **One day before return**, an automatic return reminder goes out.
- **One day after departure**, an automatic review request goes out.
