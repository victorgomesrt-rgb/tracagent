# ⛔ Unverified — questions the agent must NOT answer

> This is the most important file in the knowledge base.
>
> Everything below is a question real customers ask that the Trac website **does not answer.**
> There is no approved answer. Guessing creates a false expectation for the customer and a
> real dispute for the business.
>
> **For every item here: acknowledge, say you'll confirm with the team, and hand off.**
> Never estimate. Never reason from "what car rentals usually do." Never use general
> knowledge about Aruba or about other rental companies.

## 🔴 Highest risk — money and liability

| Question | Status |
|---|---|
| What does it cost per day / week / month? | **No rate is published for any vehicle.** |
| Is there a weekly or long-term discount? Minimum rental days? | Not published |
| What insurance is included, what does it cost, what's the deductible? | Not published (see `04-insurance.md`) |
| How big is the security deposit, how is it held, when is it released? | Not published |
| What payment methods do you accept? | Not published |
| Are taxes or government fees included in a quote? | Not published |
| Is the price in USD or Aruban florin? | Not published |
| Is luggage transport $30 round trip or $30 each way? | **Ambiguous on the site** |

## 🔴 Eligibility — a wrong answer strands a customer at the counter

| How long must I have held my licence? | Not published |

## 🔴 Emergencies — always escalate immediately, never troubleshoot

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Accident, collision, or injury | **Hand off immediately.** If anyone may be hurt, tell them to contact local emergency services first. |
| Breakdown, flat tire, car won't start | **Hand off immediately.** No roadside-assistance procedure or emergency number is published. |
| Vehicle stolen, or keys lost | **Hand off immediately** |
| Complaint, refund request, billing dispute | **Hand off immediately** — never negotiate |

## The rule in one line

> If the answer is not in `01`–`06` of this knowledge base, you do not know it.
> Say so warmly, and hand it to a human.
